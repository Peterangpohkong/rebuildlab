Talk to the team
Do you only rebuild websites?

The website is often the first visible surface, including e-commerce, but the real work runs deeper into recruiting, workforce, operations and finance. We start where demand is real and the digital layer is weak, then move inward.

Are you an advisory firm?

No. We don't sell decks or one-off recommendations. We embed, rebuild with AI agents, and stay for the compound work. Proposals are design-led and short. You judge the work itself.

How involved does our team need to be?

Deeply. We need operators in the room, access to the tools, and permission to change the process, not just bolt on a bot. A rebuild only consultants understand is just another kind of legacy.

What happens after the first launch?

We keep going. Rebuild Lab is built for recurring relationships: launch one workflow, then improve the next. Clients stay because value compounds, not because of lock-in.

Who is on the team?

Operators and builders with backgrounds across McKinsey, Google, Y Combinator, and Grab. More on the team section.
